Important Information

These reminders will help you move through the day smoothly while onsite.

Meal Tickets

Meal tickets must be presented at the Dining Room, with the exception of participants during the lunch hour only. Their wristband will gain them entry.

All others need meal tickets to enter, and participants will also need a meal ticket for dinner.

Non-Participants

Non-participants onsite must be registered and accounted for. The fee is $5 per person.

They will have a different color wristband than participants and must wear their wristband for entry into performances.

If you did not pre-register, please visit the Info Booth in A7.
